GOT QUESTIONS?

Let's clear things up.

Everything you should know before getting started.

SKIPQ is a hospital appointment and queue management platform designed to connect patients, doctors and hospital administrators.
Create an account, explore departments, select a doctor and an available slot, and submit an appointment request. The hospital confirms it according to its booking policy.
A digital token identifies your place in a hospital's appointment or queue system after the relevant booking is confirmed.
No. Its symptom-navigation feature is intended to help explore relevant departments. It does not diagnose conditions or replace medical advice.
SKIPQ's fairness principle is based on transparent published booking rules, not paid queue jumping. Clinical emergencies and legally required accessibility arrangements must follow documented hospital policies.
Live tracking requires an integrated hospital queue system that continuously updates token and appointment information. A disconnected demonstration page cannot provide live queue information.
The production application must use secure authentication, appropriate backend permissions and privacy controls. Review the hospital's privacy policy before sharing personal information.
