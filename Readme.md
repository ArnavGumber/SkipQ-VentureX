## About SKIPQ

**SKIPQ** is an intelligent healthcare queue management platform designed to simplify hospital appointments, reduce unnecessary waiting, and improve coordination between patients, doctors, and hospital administrators.

The platform aims to replace inefficient manual queue-management processes with a transparent digital system where patients can discover doctors, book appointments, receive digital tokens, and track their queue status.

SKIPQ focuses on making healthcare more organized, accessible, and patient-friendly.

## The Problem

Patients frequently experience long queues and unnecessary waiting at hospitals and clinics.

Traditional appointment and queue-management processes often result in:

- Long waiting periods and overcrowded waiting areas.
- Uncertainty regarding appointment timings.
- Difficulty tracking queue progress.
- Inefficient coordination between doctors and patients.
- Manual administrative workload for hospital staff.

These challenges affect both patient experience and hospital operational efficiency.

## Our Solution

SKIPQ proposes an integrated digital platform that connects patients, doctors, and hospital administrators through a centralized appointment and queue-management system.

### Key Features

**1. User Authentication**
- Secure registration and login for patients and hospital staff.
- Role-based access to relevant platform features.

**2. Doctor Discovery**
- Browse available hospital departments.
- Explore and select doctors.

**3. Smart Slot Booking**
- View doctor availability.
- Schedule appointments conveniently.

**4. Digital Queue Generation**
- Generate unique digital token numbers for appointments.
- Organize patients into structured queues.

**5. Live Queue Tracking**
- Monitor queue position.
- View estimated waiting times.
- Stay informed about queue progress.

**6. Notifications**
- Receive appointment reminders.
- Get notified about upcoming turns and queue updates.

**7. Admin Dashboard**
- Manage doctors and departments.
- Monitor appointments and patient records.
- Simplify hospital queue administration.

## How SKIPQ Works

1. A patient registers or logs into SKIPQ.
2. The patient browses available departments and doctors.
3. The patient selects a suitable doctor and appointment slot.
4. SKIPQ generates a digital queue token.
5. The patient monitors their queue position and estimated waiting time.
6. Notifications keep the patient informed about appointment and queue updates.
7. Hospital administrators manage the system through a centralized dashboard.

## Technology & Architecture

SKIPQ is developed using web technologies and cloud-based infrastructure.

| Component | Technology |
|---|---|
| Frontend | HTML5, CSS3, Vanilla JavaScript |
| Backend | Node.js, Express.js |
| Database | Firebase Firestore |
| Authentication | Firebase Authentication |
| Real-time Updates | Firestore Real-time Listeners |
| Deployment | Render |

The architecture is designed to support responsive access across smartphones, tablets, and desktop computers.
